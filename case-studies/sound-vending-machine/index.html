Sound Vending Machine

A playful vending machine for unexpected songs and small exchanges between strangers

  • 3D Interaction
  • Creative Coding
  • Music Discovery
  • Interaction Design
  • Vibe Coding

My role

Design & Development

Experience highlights

Tactile coin-and-knob interaction · collectible music cans · a hidden Song Exchange with custom can designs

Inspired by street-side vending machines and the small thrill of stumbling upon a song you unexpectedly love, I reimagined music discovery as a tactile, playful ritual: insert a coin, make a selection, turn the knob, and watch a music can drop from the machine.

Every song is packaged in its own collectible can with a unique visual identity and a tiny “serving suggestion” — drink before summer ends, open after midnight, or take it with you on the way home. Rather than telling you exactly what you should listen to, the experience is designed to create a little musical serendipity.

Hidden inside the machine is a discoverable Song Exchange. You can take a song left by a stranger, then customize a new can with colors, patterns, stickers, and a handwritten mark before leaving your own song behind for the next person.

Two strangers briefly meet through a vending machine — by exchanging just one song.

我的工作

设计与开发

体验要点

投币与旋钮交互 · 独立设计的音乐罐头 · 隐藏的歌曲互换与罐身创作

灵感来自街头自动贩卖机，以及偶然发现一首好歌时的惊喜。我把「音乐推荐」重新设计成了一次可以触摸和参与的小游戏：用户投币、选择、旋转旋钮，一只音乐罐头便会从机器中掉下来。

每首歌都被装进拥有独立视觉设计的罐子，并附上一句属于它的「饮用说明」——请在夏天结束前饮用、午夜后开启、适合在回家的路上慢慢喝。 比起精准地告诉你“应该听什么”，我更想创造一次意料之外的音乐偶遇。

网页中还藏着一个可以被发现的 「互换」机制：用户可以带走陌生人留下的一首歌，再通过颜色、图案、贴纸与手绘痕迹亲手制作一只新的罐子，把自己的歌留给下一个人。

两个互不认识的人，就这样借一台自动贩卖机，短暂地交换了一首歌。
