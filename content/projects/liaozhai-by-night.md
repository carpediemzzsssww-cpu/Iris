## English

You have wandered into an old scroll painting at night. Wherever your finger or cursor goes, the lantern goes, and the painting shows itself in its light. Tap the scroll and a pale gold ripple spreads from the spot; it turns vermilion when you have found something. Linger where the fireflies gather, and one of the strange tales hidden in the painting steps forward.

There are three scrolls and nine encounters, and each scroll has its own soundscape. Every encounter you meet is written into a small record book. Finish a scroll and it is stamped with that story's seal. Record all nine and the end of the scroll finally opens: Pu Songling's own preface to Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio.

On a phone, two fingers pan across the wide painting, so no corner is out of reach, and a guide scroll unrolls on your first visit. Encounters stay found, so you can come back another night and keep walking.

## 中文

你误入一幅夜色古卷。指尖或鼠标移到哪里，灯就提到哪里，画在灯下一点点显出来。轻叩画面，落点漾开一圈淡金，遇到奇遇就转成朱红；在萤光聚集的地方多停一会儿，藏在画里的故事会自己现身。

全作三卷画、九段奇遇，每一卷有自己的声音。遇见的故事会记进「志异录」；一卷遇齐，画上钤一枚这一卷的故事印；九遇全部收录，卷末的跋才会打开，那是蒲松龄的《聊斋自志》。

手机上可以双指移动画卷，长卷的每个角落都走得到；第一次来，会先展开一卷指南。已经遇见的不会丢，改天回来，接着夜行。
