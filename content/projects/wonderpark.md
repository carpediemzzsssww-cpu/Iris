## English

Idea Wonderpark brings ideas and visual experiments into a low-poly 3D park. Browsing becomes an invitation to wander, with changing daylight, sunset, nighttime lighting, and sound setting the pace.

The public demo brings together concept studies, a small learning archive, generative visuals, photographs, and an abstract regional globe. Dolly acts as a guide along the way.

It explores how a collection of content can become a place: something you navigate through atmosphere and curiosity, while still finding a way into the ideas behind each stop.

## 中文

Idea Wonderpark 把想法和视觉实验放进一座低多边形 3D 公园。白天、夕阳、夜晚与声音的变化，让浏览多了一点漫游的节奏。

公开演示版收录概念案例、学习档案、生成视觉、摄影与抽象区域地球仪，并由 Dolly 作为向导。

这个项目探索的是：一组内容怎样成为一个可以逛的地方，让人顺着氛围和好奇心前进，也能找到每一站背后的想法。
