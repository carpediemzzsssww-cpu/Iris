About

From literature to AI products — building with humanities intuition and technical craft.

Chinese Lit major, building AI. I don't find this strange anymore.

I'm an AI product builder with a literature background — I understand users the way you close-read a text, and I understand the world by building things.

I still read products like novels. Slowly, and twice.

My path: Chinese Literature & Communication Studies at Wuhan University, exchange semester at Université Paris Nanterre across 16 countries, and now building AI products at Bestcem (B2B CEM SaaS). Each phase taught me something different — literature gave me sensitivity to language and human nuance, media work taught me platform logic, independent projects proved I can ship from zero, and enterprise SaaS showed me how products survive in the real world.

Wuhan · Paris · back to Wuhan. Most of what I learned was between places.

  • LLM Application Development: RAG pipelines (FAISS + embeddings), Function Calling agents, prompt architecture design, multi-model API integration (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Doubao, Anthropic)
  • Full-Stack Prototyping: React/Next.js + Python + RPA — I build working demos, not just mockups
  • Data Pipeline Engineering: Built a 30k+ entry VOC pipeline across 9 platforms with automated collection and cleaning

How I Work

  • Understand "why" before touching "how" — the problem matters more than the solution
  • MVP first, iterate fast — ship early, learn from real users, polish what matters
  • Quantify everything, then find the story — data first, narrative gives it meaning
